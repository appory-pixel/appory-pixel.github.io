Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

This policy applies to the apps published under the Appory name by Nguyen Le Bui Thao ("we", "us"). It explains what happens to your information when you use them. Not every app uses every service described here: the Our apps section lists what each app uses.

The short version

Your photos, documents, scripts, recordings and other content are processed on your device and are not uploaded to us. No account registration is required. Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Optional analytics and advertising measurement are used only if you choose them, and advertising identifiers are used only if you also allow Apple's tracking request.

Your content

Photos, documents, scans, scripts, videos, text and other content that you choose in our apps are processed only on your device. They are never uploaded to us. Temporary copies are kept in the app's temporary storage, which iOS clears automatically.

Camera, microphone and photo library

The camera and microphone are used only when you use a feature that needs them, such as scanning a document or recording a video. What you capture stays on your device unless you share it yourself. Apps that save videos to your photo library ask for permission to add items to it and do not read your existing photos.

Files you import

When you import a file (such as a text, PDF or Word document), the app reads its text on your device to create your content. The file is not uploaded to us.

Web pages

When an app loads a web address you enter, the page is loaded directly from that website, which may collect information under its own privacy policy. We do not record the addresses you visit.

Purchases

Subscriptions and other purchases are made through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your payment details.

Apps that use RevenueCat send it purchase and subscription information, a randomly generated app user identifier, and technical information needed to provide its service. We also send Apple's AdServices attribution token to RevenueCat so it can request Apple Ads attribution data from Apple and associate that data with subscription revenue. If you allow Apple's tracking request, Apple may provide more detailed attribution, including when you interacted with an ad; if you decline, only standard attribution is requested. RevenueCat does not receive your content or payment card details. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Optional Google Analytics and ad measurement

Apps that use Google Analytics for Firebase ask for your choice under Privacy choices. If you choose App analytics, Google receives app activity (such as onboarding steps, which features you use, paywall views, purchase outcomes and error codes), purchase events, an app instance identifier, and information about your device and app. Google may use IP addresses to derive approximate location. We do not send your content, file names, web addresses, typed text, names or email addresses to Google, and we have disabled collection of the identifier for vendor (IDFV).

If you also choose Measure our Google ads and allow Apple's tracking request, Google may use advertising identifiers and event data to measure our advertising. If you also choose Personalised ads, Google may use this data to build advertising audiences and show you ads for our apps in other apps and on websites (remarketing). See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner sites and apps.

Crash reports

Apps that use Firebase Crashlytics send crash reports only if you choose App analytics under Privacy choices. A crash report contains technical information such as the app version, device model, operating system version, the state of the app when it crashed, and an installation identifier. It does not contain your content. See Firebase privacy information.

Your choices

Declining optional data never affects the app's features, Premium access or purchases. You can change or withdraw your choices at any time in the app under Settings > Privacy choices, and manage Apple's permission in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. To ask about this processing or to request deletion of data held for one of our apps, email support.appory@gmail.com.

Settings

Your preferences in our apps are stored only on your device.

Children

Our apps are not directed at children. We do not ask children to provide contact information.

Our apps

Quick Print

Prints photos, documents, scans, web pages and text using Apple's AirPrint. Print jobs go from your device straight to the printer you select; we never see what you print. Uses: Apple App Store, RevenueCat, and optional Google Analytics for Firebase.

Teleprompter

Shows your script on screen while you record video with the camera and microphone. Scripts and videos are stored only on your device; videos are saved to your photo library only when you choose to. Uses: Apple App Store, RevenueCat, and optional Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
